How it works

  1. Tap Choose photos and select photos from your gallery or files.
  2. Wait for each photo to show Ready.
  3. Tap Save. On a phone, choose Save Image (iPhone) or save to your gallery. On a computer the JPG goes to Downloads.
  4. Open Project Photos and attach the saved JPG as usual.

Your photos

    Avoid converting next time

    You can make your phone save photos as JPG from the start:

    • iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → choose Most Compatible. New photos are saved as JPG. Photos already taken stay HEIC.
    • Android: most phones already save JPG. If yours saves HEIF, open the Camera app → Settings → Picture formats and turn HEIF off.
    • Screenshots are usually PNG, so they always need converting here.
